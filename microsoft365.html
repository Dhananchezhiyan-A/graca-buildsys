Cutover & Staged Migration

Core Microsoft 365 Services

Exchange Online

Enterprise-grade hosted email with 50 GB mailboxes, anti-spam and anti-malware protection, shared mailboxes, distribution groups, mail flow rules, and mobile device sync via ActiveSync.

Microsoft Teams

Unified communication hub for chat, video meetings, screen sharing, and file collaboration. We configure teams, channels, guest access policies, meeting policies, and Teams Phone integration.

OneDrive for Business

Secure personal cloud storage with 1 TB per user, file versioning, sharing controls, and sync across devices. Ideal for individual file management with enterprise governance and retention policies.

SharePoint Online

Team sites, document libraries, intranet portals, and workflow automation. We design information architecture, permission structures, and content management policies for your organization.

Licensing Management

Right-size your license portfolio with ongoing audits, license assignment optimization, true-up planning, and cost analysis. We ensure you pay only for what you need across all M365 SKUs.

Security & Compliance

Multi-factor authentication, conditional access, data loss prevention (DLP), retention policies, eDiscovery, and compliance center configuration to meet regulatory and industry requirements.
