Firewall Solutions

Next-generation firewalls that inspect, filter, and protect all network traffic at the perimeter and within your internal network.

Next-Gen Firewall (NGFW)

Deploy Fortinet FortiGate, Sophos XG, and Cisco Meraki next-generation firewalls with deep packet inspection, application-aware filtering, intrusion prevention (IPS), and advanced threat protection at your network perimeter.

Web Filtering & DNS Security

Block access to malicious websites, phishing domains, and inappropriate content with category-based web filtering. DNS-layer security stops threats before they reach your network with real-time threat intelligence feeds.

VPN & Remote Access

Secure SSL/IPsec VPN for remote workers and site-to-site connectivity between branch offices. Multi-factor authentication integration and split-tunneling policies for secure remote access without compromising performance.

  • Firewall rule audit, optimization, and compliance documentation
  • High-availability (HA) clustering for zero-downtime failover
  • SD-WAN integration for branch office security and connectivity
  • 24×7 firewall monitoring, log analysis, and threat alerting

Endpoint Security

Protect every laptop, desktop, and server with advanced endpoint detection, response, and management.

Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)

Deploy Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, CrowdStrike, or Sophos Intercept X for real-time threat detection, behavioral analysis, and automated response on all endpoints. Detect and isolate compromised devices before threats spread across your network.

Device Management & Encryption

Centralized endpoint management with policy enforcement, BitLocker/device encryption, USB port control, application whitelisting, and patch management. Ensure every device meets your security baseline before accessing corporate resources.

Anti-Malware

Real-time scanning, behavioral detection, and sandbox analysis for zero-day threats.

Device Control

USB, Bluetooth, and peripheral restrictions to prevent data exfiltration.

Patch Management

Automated OS and application patching with compliance reporting and rollback.

Email Security

Stop phishing, spam, malware, and business email compromise before they reach your users' inboxes.

Anti-Phishing

AI-powered phishing detection with URL rewriting, sandbox detonation, and impersonation protection against BEC attacks.

Spam Filtering

Outbound Filtering

Security Awareness

Cloud Security

Secure your cloud environments, SaaS applications, and hybrid infrastructure against misconfigurations and unauthorized access.

Microsoft 365 Security

Conditional access policies, MFA enforcement, Safe Attachments, Safe Links, anti-phishing policies, and Microsoft Defender for Office 365 configuration for comprehensive cloud email and collaboration security.

Google Workspace Security

Context-aware access, security center monitoring, advanced phishing protection, data loss prevention, and Vault retention policies for Google Workspace environments.

Cloud Access Security (CASB)

Discover and control shadow IT, enforce data policies across SaaS applications, monitor cloud app usage, and prevent unauthorized data sharing in cloud storage platforms.

Backup Solutions

Automated, encrypted backup for servers, endpoints, email, and cloud data with rapid recovery capabilities.

Server & VM Backup

Image-level and file-level backup for physical servers and virtual machines (VMware, Hyper-V) with incremental forever technology, encryption at rest, and offsite replication to secure cloud storage.

Endpoint Backup

Automated backup of laptop and desktop data including documents, emails, and application data. Protect against ransomware, hardware failure, and accidental deletion with point-in-time recovery.

Cloud & SaaS Backup

Backup Microsoft 365 (Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams), Google Workspace, and Salesforce data independently of vendor retention — protecting against accidental deletion, malicious insiders, and ransomware.

  • 3-2-1 backup strategy — three copies, two media types, one offsite
  • Automated backup verification and restore testing
  • Immutable backup storage resistant to ransomware encryption
  • Retention policies aligned with compliance requirements

Disaster Recovery

Business continuity planning and disaster recovery solutions to minimize downtime and data loss during catastrophic events.

Recovery Time & Point Objectives

We design disaster recovery plans with defined RTO (Recovery Time Objective) and RPO (Recovery Point Objective) targets based on your business criticality analysis. From file-level recovery in minutes to full-site failover in hours — we match the solution to your tolerance for downtime.

DR Testing & Documentation

Quarterly disaster recovery drills with documented runbooks, failover/failback procedures, and post-test reports. Ensure your team knows exactly what to do when disaster strikes — because untested backups are not backups.
