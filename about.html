Company Overview

GRACA BUILDSYS LLP is a Chennai-based enterprise IT solutions company built on a simple promise: deliver technology that works reliably, scales with your business, and is supported by people you can reach when it matters most.

Founded and led by Sugunathan Kumar, we partner with organizations across manufacturing, healthcare, education, finance, retail, and professional services — helping them modernize productivity platforms, strengthen infrastructure, secure digital assets, and adopt intelligent automation.

From Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace deployments to enterprise networking, firewall management, and AI-driven workflow automation, we provide end-to-end IT services under one accountable partner. Our approach combines technical depth with transparent communication, competitive pricing, and SLA-backed support that keeps your operations running without interruption.

Whether you are migrating email, rolling out collaboration tools across multiple branches, or building a secure foundation for growth, GRACA BUILDSYS LLP brings the expertise, responsiveness, and long-term commitment your business deserves.

Our Journey

From a focused IT services practice to a full-spectrum enterprise solutions partner trusted by businesses across South India.

2018
Foundation

GRACA BUILDSYS LLP was established in Chennai with a focus on business email solutions, desktop support, and small-business IT consulting under the leadership of Sugunathan Kumar.

2019 – 2020
Cloud Productivity Expansion

Expanded into Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace deployments, helping clients migrate from on-premise email to secure, cloud-based collaboration platforms with zero-downtime planning.

2021 – 2022
Infrastructure & Networking Growth

Added enterprise networking, server solutions, firewall deployments, and comprehensive AMC contracts — serving multi-location clients in manufacturing, healthcare, and education sectors.

2023 – 2024
Security & Compliance Focus

Strengthened cybersecurity offerings with endpoint protection, email security, backup solutions, and disaster recovery planning to meet growing compliance and data protection requirements.

2025 – Present
AI & Intelligent Automation

Launched AI solutions and workflow automation services, enabling clients to streamline operations with chatbots, generative AI integration, and custom business process automation.
